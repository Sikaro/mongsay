Clear, candid answers regarding broker rebate mechanics, safety of funds, and institutional clearing.

When you trade through a broker, the broker charges a commission or spread mark-up. As an institutional partner, SAGFX receives wholesale volume clearing compensation from the broker. Rather than keeping all fees, SAGFX passes up to 85% of this revenue directly back to you as weekly cashback. You trade identical conditions, but recover significant transaction costs.

Your spreads, execution speeds, and fill conditions are identical (or superior) to opening an account directly with the prime broker. Our cashback comes entirely from institutional volume share, not by inflating your costs.

We maintain clearing partnerships with top tier-1 regulated prime brokers, including IC Markets (ASIC/FSA), Vantage Markets (ASIC/CIMA), Exness (FCA/CySEC), and FP Markets (ASIC/CySEC). Traders can link existing accounts or deploy new sub-accounts under the SAGFX whitelist.

All trading volume is calculated on Sunday midnight GMT. Cashback is credited automatically every Monday directly to your USDT (TRC20/BEP20) crypto wallet, Cambodian local bank transfer (ABA / Bakong), or routed back into your MT4/MT5 trading account balance with zero withdrawal delay.
